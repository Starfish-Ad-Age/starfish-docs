# Media page

The **Media** page keeps everything Starfish has made for you. Open **Media** in the sidebar.

There are no separate image or video pages. You make images and videos in a chat; see [Image Generation](/media/image-generation) and [Video Generation](/media/video-generation). Slide decks are made in [Studio](/studio/overview).

## Tabs

- **Designs** -- files Starfish made in chats, such as HTML pages, PDFs and CSVs.
- **Images** -- every image made in a chat.
- **Videos** -- every clip made in a chat.

## Open an item

- Click a design to open the chat it was made in, with the file shown.
- Click an image or video to open it in its chat.
- Images and videos from earlier versions of Starfish, made before generation moved into chat, open in a viewer on the Media page.

## Free up space

Images and videos are stored on your Mac. To see how much space they use and delete old ones, open **Settings → Storage**. See [Storage](/settings/storage).
